## 01 会撒谎的进度条 / The Lying Progress Bar

永远到不了 100% 的进度条——走到 99% 就心虚倒退。
A progress bar that never reaches 100% — it panics at 99% and retreats.

关于 loading 体验里的诚实问题。
On honesty in loading experiences.

## 02 撤回 AI / Retracted AI

接入真实 AI（Puter.js）。它给你上头的恋爱脑建议，3 秒后逐字撤回，不留证据。
Powered by a real AI (Puter.js). It gives you dramatic, love-brained advice — then deletes it word by word after 3 seconds. No evidence left.

关于 AI "有权回答、无需负责" 的讽刺。
A satire on AI's power to answer without the duty to be accountable.

**设计迭代 / Design iterations:**
文案库随机抽 → 真实 AI 生成；人格尝试：毒舌朋友 → 官僚 → 恋爱脑（定稿）
Scripted responses → real AI generation; persona tests: brutally honest friend → bureaucrat → hopeless romantic (final)
